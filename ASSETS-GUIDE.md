# Zanzibar Soap — Assets Guide
Drop your files into the folders below. The website auto-loads them by filename.

## Videos
| File | What to film |
|------|-------------|
| `videos/hero-bg.mp4` | Zanzibar coastline, spice market, or soap-making montage — plays silently as hero background |
| `videos/soap-making-process.mp4` | Step-by-step soap making — shown in the "Watch How We Make It" section |

Also add `images/hero-bg.jpg` and `images/process-poster.jpg` as fallback/thumbnail images.
