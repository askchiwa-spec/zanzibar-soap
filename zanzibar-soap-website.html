Our Story

It's Not Just Soap.
It's Our Pride.

There's something special about Zanzibar. Its spices, its ancient trade routes that carried the finest botanicals across the world. For us, it was never just a name — it was the inspiration behind everything we make.

We are a Tanzanian beauty company, manufacturing 100% natural skincare products that are affordable, honest, and deeply effective. Every product is a tribute to the richness of our land and a commitment to the health of your skin.
