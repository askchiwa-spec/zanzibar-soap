Introduction / Utangulizi

About Zanzibar Soap

Read both the English and Kiswahili sections to fully understand our company mission and values.

English

Zanzibar Soap Limited is a Tanzanian skincare company specializing in natural soaps and personal care products — including beauty soaps, scrubs, body oils, body wash, hand wash, body balm, and daily skincare essentials.


We combine natural ingredients with modern production standards to create high-quality products inspired by the beauty and heritage of Zanzibar and Africa. Our focus is providing cleanliness, freshness, skin care, and a luxurious experience using carefully selected ingredients such as turmeric, honey, shea butter, coffee, and natural oils.


We serve both local and export markets while building a trusted African skincare brand.

Kiswahili

Zanzibar Soap Limited ni kampuni ya Kitanzania inayojishughulisha na utengenezaji wa sabuni za asili na bidhaa za utunzaji wa ngozi — ikiwemo sabuni za urembo, scrub, mafuta ya mwili, body wash, hand wash, body balm, na bidhaa za matumizi ya kila siku.

Staff Conduct / Mwenendo wa Wafanyakazi

Customer Service
Standards

How we treat customers reflects who we are as a brand. Every interaction is an opportunity to build trust.

🤝
Respect every customer — Greet warmly, listen carefully, and treat all customers with equal dignity regardless of how much they buy.
🗣️
Explain products clearly — Use simple language. Describe what a product does and which skin concern it addresses. Offer to show ingredients if asked.
🚫
Do not exaggerate product benefits — Be honest. We say "helps brighten skin" — not "will make you completely white." Trust is built through honesty, not promises.
📦
Know your stock — Be aware of which products are available so you never promise something out of stock. Direct customers to alternatives confidently.
📱
WhatsApp orders — For online orders, direct customers to WhatsApp: +255 798 538 366. Confirm product name, size, and delivery address.
🎁
Wholesale inquiries — Refer hotels, shops, or bulk buyers to the manager. We offer special rates for wholesale and hotel orders.
Hygiene & Safety / Usafi na Usalama

Hygiene & Safety Rules

We sell cleanliness. We must model cleanliness. These rules are non-negotiable.

🧼
Wash hands before handling any product — This applies every time, without exception. Dirty hands contaminate products and damage our reputation.
🧹
Keep products away from dirt and dust — Store products in clean, dry areas. Wipe display surfaces daily. Never place products on the ground.
👕
Wear clean clothes at all times — You represent the brand. Clean, neat appearance shows professionalism and respect for customers.
🌡️
Store products correctly — Keep away from direct sunlight and extreme heat. Cool, dry storage preserves product quality and shelf life.
📋
Check expiry and condition — Never sell a damaged, leaking, or expired product. Report any such items to the manager immediately.
